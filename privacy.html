Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

At TNL Trader Academy ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible via tnltrader.com, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any user information processed through our platform, services, Discord integrations, and webhook systems.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide our trading alerts, educational community, and integration services:

  • Account Details: Email addresses and payment references processed securely through our third-party checkout partners (e.g., Whop, Stripe). We never store raw credit card information.
  • Integration Identifiers: Webhook URLs (Discord, TradingView, PineConnector) used to automate signal routing and notifications to your channels.
  • Usage Logs: System logs tracking webhook payload dispatch statuses, error handling, and command execution for diagnostic and performance purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly utilized to deliver community services, including:

  • Dispatching real-time market intelligence, 7-Gate alerts, and bias updates.
  • Managing VIP community membership access across Discord and Telegram.
  • Monitoring bot integrations, health checks, and server performance.
  • Providing technical support and billing inquiries.

3. Data Protection & Integrations

We store webhook URLs and configuration tokens securely using environment-level encryption. We do not sell, lease, or share your private webhook endpoints, contact details, or account data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with third-party software including Discord, Telegram, TradingView, Whop, and AWS host servers. Your interactions with these tools are governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request the deletion of your account metadata and webhook configurations at any time by contacting our support team or clearing integration keys within your profile settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, reach out to our team at support@tnltrader.com or via our official Discord community.
