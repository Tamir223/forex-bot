FAQ
Where do the signals come from?
TNL Trader generates its own signals — it isn't relaying calls from other providers. Every signal is the output of the same 7-gate check run automatically against live price data.
Which pairs does it cover?
XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, and USDJPY — each with its own session windows and confirmation thresholds.
Do I have to execute manually?
Yes. All signals are delivered straight to Discord for you to review and trade manually.
What happens during major news events?
New signals pause automatically around high-impact releases like FOMC, CPI, and NFP, then resume once the window clears.

No. Signals are standardized and delivered the same way to every member — they aren't tailored to your account, risk tolerance, or goals. Trading involves substantial risk of loss, and the decision to act on any signal is yours alone.
